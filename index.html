TikTok Downloader

Paste a TikTok link and get a clean download URL.

How to use TikTok Downloader

  1. Find the video: Open TikTok and go to the video you want.
  2. Copy the link: Tap “Share” → “Copy link” on the video.
  3. Paste the link: Come back here and paste the TikTok URL into the box above.
  4. Generate download link: Click Get Download Link. A direct link will appear below.
  5. Download: Click the generated link. Your browser will open the video and let you save it.
